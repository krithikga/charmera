What is CHARMERA?

The KODAK CHARMERA is a tiny digital camera supporting both photo taking and video recording. Customised KODAK frames, vintage filters and date stamp are available in the photo mode. A total of 7 retro styles are sold in blind boxes, adding fun and collectibility for you and your friends!

With media transfer via the included USB-C cable, it's easy to move your memories from camera to device and share them in a snap. A package includes a camera, a keyring and a USB-C cable. Micro SD card is NOT included.
