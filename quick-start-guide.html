Taking Photos
1
Insert memory card

Insert a Micro SD card (1GB–128GB) into the memory card slot on the back of the device.

2
Power on

Press and hold the On/Off button until the indicator light activates.

3
Select a frame or filter

Use the Up/Down buttons to browse frames and filters. The remaining shot count is shown in the upper right corner of the screen.

4
Press the shutter

Press the shutter button to take a photo. For video, switch to video mode using the Play/OK button and press the shutter to start and stop recording.

Taking Videos
1
Switch to video mode

Use the Up/Down buttons to navigate to video recording mode.

2
Start recording

Press the shutter button to begin. An indicator on screen will show the recording is active.

3
Stop recording

Press the shutter button again to stop. Your video will be saved automatically in AVI format at 30fps.

Connecting to a Device
1
Insert memory card

Ensure a Micro SD card is properly inserted into the Charmera before connecting.

2
Connect via USB-C

Use a USB-C cable to connect the Charmera to your computer. The device will be automatically detected.

3
Access your files

The Charmera will appear as a removable drive on your computer. Open it to review, copy, and share your photos and videos.
