FAQ

Charmera Functions How to Use Faulty Charmera Privacy Policy

What functions does the Charmera offer?

The Charmera supports photo taking & video recording, featuring 4 frames & 7 filters in photo mode.

How do I transfer photos & videos from the Charmera?

Ensure a memory card is inserted into the Charmera. Connect the Charmera to a computer for easy review, copying & sharing.

How can I check how many photos I can take?

The upper right corner of the screen displays the remaining number of photos available for capture.

What memory card does the Charmera support?

The Charmera supports Micro SD cards ranging from 1GB up to 128GB.

What image and video formats does the Charmera use?

Photos are saved in JPEG format at 1440 × 1080 resolution. Videos are saved in AVI format at 30 fps.

How do I turn on the Charmera?

Press and hold the On/Off button located on the top of the device until the indicator light activates.

How do I charge the Charmera?

Connect the Charmera to a power source using a USB-C cable. The indicator light will show the charging status.

How do I insert a memory card?

Insert a compatible Micro SD card (1GB–128GB) into the memory card slot on the back of the device.

How do I navigate menus?

Use the Up/Down buttons on the back of the device to navigate. Press Play/OK to confirm your selection.

My Charmera won't turn on. What should I do?

Charge the device fully using a USB-C cable. If it still doesn't respond, press the Reset button on the back of the device with a pin or toothpick.

My photos aren't saving. What's wrong?

Ensure a compatible Micro SD card is properly inserted. Check that the card is not full or corrupted. Try formatting the card on a computer and reinserting it.

The screen isn't displaying anything.

Try resetting the device. If the issue persists, please contact our support team via the Customer Support page.

How do I contact support for a faulty unit?

Email us at shopeexcharmera@becharmed.com or use the Contact Us form on the support page.
