Sustainability

In 2025, Charmera was assessed by EcoVadis, one of the world’s most trusted providers of sustainability ratings for businesses.

Charmera was awarded the Bronze EcoVadis Medal, placing the company among the top 35% of all organizations evaluated globally over the past 12 months.

This recognition reflects Charmera’s ongoing commitment to responsible business practices and continuous improvement.

Sustainability Goals

Waste

Approach zero waste at Charmera sites worldwide. 99% overall waste diversion from landfill and incineration by 2025.

Progress: Achieved. 99% of waste generated at Charmera sites in 2023 was diverted from disposal via landfill or incineration (this does not include waste-to-energy diversion).

Water

Reduce water withdrawal from worldwide Charmera operations by 25% by 2025.

Progress: Charmera has successfully met its goal, achieving a 31% reduction in water withdrawal in 2023, ahead of the 2025 target.

Greenhouse Gas

Reduce GHG emissions from worldwide operations by 25% by 2025.

Progress: Achieved. Charmera sites have exceeded this goal with a 56% reduction in GHG emissions in 2023 compared to the baseline year of 2016.

Recycling

Triple the quantity of spent solvents recovered by Charmera from external sources by 2025.

Progress: Ongoing 106% increase in quantity recovered in 2023 compared to 2016.
